Verdict

Forwarding, fallback and no-fallback semantics all behave correctly. The only genuine divergence in 24h is a repeating pattern of SB 500 + timeout bursts on /v1/eligibilitycheck, traced end-to-end to cold stubbed downstreams tripping SB's circuit breakers ~12–15 min after each env redeploy under load-batch traffic. Play absorbs the identical downstream slowness without tripping. The Play fallback absorbed every SB failure — end-to-end user impact ≈ zero in this window.
Prod-relevant asymmetry: under downstream slowness SB converts it into fail-fast 500s (circuit opens → 500) where Play degrades into slow 200s. Fallback covers allowlisted paths — but /internal/verifyReceipt is no_fallback, so an SB-only regression there would leak to real users.

Caveat: this pattern is largely a stub-cold-start artifact amplified by hourly env churn — not evidence of a steady-state SB defect. Steady-state parity held (~4.9% both stacks; SB p99 better than Play: 1.78s vs 2.47s).

The causal chain — what actually happens in a burst

  1. Lockstep redeploy. Service pods and stub pods roll out minutes apart (17:08–17:10, 18:06–18:07). The whole env churns hourly.
  2. Load batch lands on cold stubs. ~12–15 min later an NFT batch ramps to ~23k req/5m. Fresh stub JVMs (RSS 137→437 MB over 30 min, ~0.1 CPU) serve slowly for both stacks — Play's downstream avg jumps 1ms → 2.0–2.7s.
  3. SB crosses its timeouts; Play doesn't. SB's Feign calls hit 15–22s p99 → 10s readTimeout exceeded → retries (up to 6 attempts, each a breaker call) → 5 consecutive failures open the resilience4j CB. Play's calls stay under its 10s callTimeout; Pekko's breaker counts failures, not slowness → stays closed.
  4. Open breaker = instant 500s. CircuitBreakerOpenException on IdentityProfileIdByIdentifier (~5.5k rejections/burst) and CatsClient (~500) → eligibilitycheck 500s → fallback_5xx back to Play. In-flight queueing to 1,000+ and FDs 700→1,665 cause Play→SB timeouts → forward_error.
  5. Self-limiting recovery. ~15 min in, stubs warm → ~1s downstream → half-open probe succeeds → CB closes. Traffic never dropped during recovery — warmup, not load shedding.

Burst 3 (~19:10) was broader: Play's ppg_notify circuit also opened and SB tripped pmEligibility / IdentityLinkAccount / getSubscriptionStatus / ppg_notify — a wider shared-downstream event, not SB-specific.

Metric defects on the board

  • application_responses double-counts on SB — ~2× vs http_server_requests_seconds_count (verifyReceipt: 50.3k vs 25.0k). All SB volume comparisons are inflated 2×; rates still valid.
  • resilience4j_bulkhead_available_concurrent_calls never moved off 100/400 despite 1,000+ in-flight — either the gauge is stale or the bulkhead never bottlenecked. Worth verifying before relying on it.
  • Instant queries on this datasource return empty; range queries are required.

Recommendations

  1. Warm the stubs / stagger redeploys before NFT measurement windows, or the parity numbers keep getting polluted by cold-start bursts.
  2. Right-size the HC5 pool (maxConnectionsPerRoute) for downstream concurrency under saturation — queueing at the pool is what pushes SB calls past the 10s readTimeout.
  3. Decide whether CB-open → 500 is acceptable parity drift on eligibilitycheck. Fallback absorbs it today; in prod a real Identity/CATS wobble would look identical.
  4. Protect /internal/verifyReceipt — the only no_fallback path. Add extra scrutiny here before ramping the prod split.
  5. Fix the application_responses double-count before the prod CR — otherwise the board reads SB error volume at 2× reality.
